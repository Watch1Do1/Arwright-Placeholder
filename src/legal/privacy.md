# Arwright Privacy Policy

**Effective date and last updated:** November 19, 2026

Arwright, LLC ("Arwright," "we," "us") is a Wyoming limited liability company (Wyoming filing ID 2026-001974171), operated from Colorado. This policy explains what we collect when you use the Arwright writing coach at app.arwrightlearning.com (the "App") and our information site at arwright-placeholder.vercel.app (the "Site"), why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

Questions? The fastest way to reach us is **hello@arwrightlearning.com**. Our mailing address is in Section 13.

---

## The short version

- **Arwright is a writing coach.** It asks questions and gives guidance. It does not write or rewrite students' work.
- **Schools:** if your school gave you Arwright, your school is in charge of your records. We work for the school.
- **Homeschool families:** the parent is in charge of the family account. The homeschool beta is for students **13 and older** only.
- **We never sell student data, never show ads, and never use student data for targeted advertising.** The App has no analytics or advertising trackers.
- **Arwright doesn't train its own AI models on student writing.** Our AI providers don't train on it either, under their commercial terms.
- **Before a student's writing goes to our AI providers,** we automatically replace the student's own name, plus email addresses and U.S. phone numbers we detect. Other details a student types may still be sent, so we ask students not to include them.
- **Parents (and schools) can see, download, correct, and ask us to delete** student information. Just email us.

## 1. Who this policy covers

- **Students** who use the App through a school or a homeschool family.
- **Parents and guardians** who run a homeschool family account.

If your school has signed a separate agreement with us (for example, a student data privacy agreement), that agreement controls how we handle your school's student data where it differs from this policy.

**Accounts that never join.** If someone creates an account but never joins a school, class, or family, we automatically delete it after 7 days.

## 3. Cookies, browser storage, and outside requests

**In the App,** we use only the browser storage needed for it to work:

- Firebase Authentication keeps you signed in (stored in your browser's IndexedDB or localStorage).
- localStorage remembers the email address you used while an email sign-in link is pending, and which paper you were working on.
- IndexedDB keeps a local copy of unsubmitted drafts so work isn't lost. Local drafts older than 7 days are deleted from your browser automatically.

The App has **no analytics, no advertising cookies, and no tracking pixels.** Its fonts and styles are served from our own hosting, and default avatars are generated inside your browser without sending anything about you to anyone.

When you create a password, your browser checks it against the Have I Been Pwned list of leaked passwords. Only the first 5 characters of a scrambled version (a hash) of the password are sent. Your password itself never leaves your browser.

**On the Site,** we use Vercel Web Analytics, which counts visits without cookies, to understand overall traffic (pages viewed, referring site, and general device, browser, and country). The Site loads fonts from Google Fonts and images from Unsplash, so those services receive your IP address and browser information when the page loads. The Site uses no advertising cookies.

## 4. How the AI coach handles writing

When a student asks the coach for help, the relevant part of their writing is sent to an AI provider so it can respond:

- **Anthropic** (Claude) is our main provider for students.
- **Microsoft Azure OpenAI** is our backup. We use a U.S. Data Zone deployment, so its processing stays in U.S. data centers.

Before text is sent, Arwright automatically replaces:

- the student's own name, as it appears on their account, including anywhere it appears in their writing;
- email addresses we detect; and
- U.S. phone numbers we detect.

This detection is automatic and may not catch everything. Other names or personal details a student types may still be sent, so we encourage students to leave them out. Dates of birth are never sent to AI providers.

**What the providers do with it.** We use each provider's standard commercial API. Neither provider trains its AI models on our data under its commercial terms.

- **Anthropic** deletes what we send, and its replies, within 30 days. The exceptions are content flagged for review under its usage policy, which may be kept for up to 2 years, and anything the law requires it to keep.
- **Microsoft Azure** may store content flagged by its automated abuse monitoring in the United States so that people at Microsoft can review it for abuse.

**Arwright doesn't train its own AI models on student writing.**

The coach is designed to guide, not to write for students. AI feedback can be wrong; students, teachers, and parents should use their own judgment.

## 6. Schools: we work for the school

When a school or district uses Arwright:

- **The school controls student records.** We collect, use, and keep student information only to provide Arwright to that school, under the school's direction.
- **School accounts collect a student's first and last name, not date of birth.** The other information is listed in Section 2.
- **FERPA.** Where the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act applies, we act as a "school official" with a legitimate educational interest (34 CFR 99.31(a)(1)(i)(B)). We stay under the school's direct control for how we use and maintain education records, and we don't re-disclose them except as the school allows or the law requires.
- **No commercial use.** We never sell student data, never use it for targeted advertising, and never build a profile of a student except to provide Arwright's educational service to that school.
- **Under-13 students.** Arwright is built for high school. If a school uses Arwright with a student under 13, we rely on the school's authorization for educational use only, as the FTC's COPPA guidance allows, and use the information for no other purpose.
- **Requests from parents and students.** Parents, and students who are 18 or older, should send requests to see, correct, or delete school records to their school. If we get one directly, we'll pass it to the school and help it respond.
- **End of the school relationship.** We delete or return the school's student data within 90 days after the school's agreement ends, or sooner if the school asks, unless the school's agreement says otherwise or the law requires us to keep it.

**Colorado public schools (Student Data Transparency and Security Act, C.R.S. 22-16).** When we serve a Colorado public school, district, or other public education entity as a school service contract provider, we:

- publish the student data elements we collect and why (the table in Section 2, which we keep current);
- use student personally identifiable information only for the purposes in our contract with that entity;
- don't sell it, don't use or share it for targeted advertising, and don't build student profiles except for school purposes the contract authorizes;
- keep a security program designed to protect it;
- tell the entity before we make a material change to how we collect, use, or share student data;
- delete it when the entity asks or when the contract ends, as the law and the contract require; and
- notify the entity as soon as possible if we discover misuse or unauthorized release of student data, including by one of our providers.

We'll give each contracting entity this list of data elements and purposes, and a list of our service providers, on request.

## 7. Homeschool families: the parent is in charge

When a parent signs up a homeschool family:

- **The parent controls the family account.** The parent adds each student, enters their name and date of birth, and shares a family code. Students join with their own email and the family code.
- **Students 13 and older only.** The App won't accept a student whose date of birth shows they are under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 in the homeschool program. If we learn we have, we'll delete it promptly. Contact us if you think this has happened.
- **The parent agrees for students under 18.** By adding a student, the parent confirms they are the student's parent or legal guardian and agrees to this policy and our Terms on the student's behalf.
- **What the parent can do.** See their students' work and activity, download each submitted paper as a PDF, correct account details, and ask us to delete a student's information or the whole family account (see Section 9).
- **FERPA and Colorado's school-contractor law generally don't apply** to a family using Arwright on its own, because no school is involved. We follow the same core practices anyway: no sale, no ads, use only to provide the service, and deletion on request.

**When a student turns 18.** The family account stays under the parent's control. A student who is 18 or older can also email us directly to:

- get a copy of their own writing;
- correct their own details; or
- ask us to delete their own writing.

If an adult student asks us to delete their own writing, we will do it, even if the parent would prefer we keep it. We'll email the parent before we delete it.

## 9. Your choices and rights

**Parents** (for students under 18 on a family account), **students 18 and older** (see Section 7), and **adult users** can ask us to:

- see the information we hold about them or their student;
- get a copy (each submitted paper can be downloaded as a PDF at any time, even while a renewal is unpaid; for anything else, email us);
- correct information that's wrong; and
- delete information or close an account.

For school accounts, please go to your school first (see Section 6).

**How to ask.** There's no delete button in the App. Email **hello@arwrightlearning.com** from the email on the account. We may need to confirm your identity before acting. We'll respond within 30 days.

**State privacy rights.** Some states, including Colorado, give residents rights to access, correct, delete, and get a copy of their personal data, and to opt out of sale, targeted advertising, and certain profiling. Arwright may not meet the size thresholds of those laws, but we offer these rights to everyone. We don't sell personal data, don't do targeted advertising, and don't use personal data for profiling that leads to legal or similarly significant decisions, so there's nothing to opt out of. If we deny a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer. We'll respond to your appeal within 45 days.

**Minors.** We know many of our users are under 18. We don't sell their data, use it for targeted advertising, or use it for profiling beyond what the writing coach itself needs, and we collect only what's needed to provide the writing coach.

We won't treat you differently for using any of these rights.

## 10. Security

We use reasonable safeguards for the sensitivity of student data. These include encrypted connections, access rules that limit who can see each student's work, dates of birth kept where only our server can read them, and providers with strong security programs. No system is perfectly secure, and we can't promise that information will never be accessed without permission.

## 11. If there's a data breach

If we learn that personal information was accessed or released without permission, we will:

- **for school data,** notify the school as soon as possible so it can meet its own duties;
- **for families and other users,** notify affected people (or a parent, for students under 18) as the law requires, including within 30 days for Colorado residents where Colorado's breach law applies; and
- tell you what happened, what information was involved, and what we're doing about it.
